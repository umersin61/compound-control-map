Who can do what?

Six reviewed Safes. Separate signing thresholds, overlapping owners and distinct contract permissions.

The key distinction

Compound Governance has explicit role-replacement and asset-revocation powers over the reviewed treasury escrow. No direct governance revocation route was identified through the reviewed Institutional Comet governor, proxy-owner, Safe-owner and enabled-module paths. A resolution alone does not install one.

All four Admin owner addresses also sit on Upgrade, meeting its 4/7 threshold together. Three Admin owners also sit on TMC: enough for Admin’s 3/4 threshold, but not TMC’s 4/6. Overlap is not proof of common custody or cooperation.

TMC Safe

0x8BacCcA3e83843555867b3865d7689D099406567

4/6 Safe controls its own custody. Holds proposer/executor roles on Treasury Timelock, allowing scheduled operations subject to delay, cancellation and destination permissions. Is tmms on Treasury escrow: initiates withdrawals specifying asset, amount and recipient, and cancels pending requests. What “controls” means here

These are identified permissions on the listed contracts, not an exhaustive inventory of every wallet, extension, strategy, module or token allowance. All Safes can normally transact with assets they hold subject to their authorization rules. This is not a full security audit.

Safe membership and main market roles: 21 September 2026. Governor, escrow, Avatar and selected treasury roles: carried forward from 19 September. Comet governor reconfirmed and source/identity review updated on 22 September. Reads were not pinned to one block. The map’s ledger records original observation dates.

Market function descriptions use reviewed core/reference source and current ABI; repository main is mutable and not a complete bytecode-matched implementation/extension audit. Escrow methods were inspected in Etherscan’s verified deployed source. Institutional pauseGuardian remains documented from deployment rather than freshly confirmed by its getter.

Historical treasury strategy permissions and WOOF streamer cancellation are described in the TMC transparency thread; current external strategy permissions and reported deprecation are not fully verified here. Assets already withdrawn cannot be recovered merely by calling escrow revoke.
