04 / A DIFFERENT OWNERSHIP MODEL

Operators run the market.
Governance controls the delegation.

CONCEPTUAL · NOT DEPLOYED

A revocable operating mandate

A governance-owned controller could preserve routine operator permissions while letting a valid governance execution replace or revoke them. The migration itself requires authorization from today’s controllers.

Upgrade delegation must be constrained: an operator must not be able to upgrade away governance’s superior authority. This is a design requirement, not an audited implementation.

ON-CHAIN VERIFIED

Treasury access-control precedent

The Governor Timelock holds the Treasury Timelock’s administrator role. That role can change delegated roles. This contrasts with the separately controlled Institutional Safes.

DOCUMENTED

Avantgarde / Zodiac precedent

A prior Compound proposal described governance ownership with scoped manager permissions through Zodiac Roles. It illustrates the separation of ownership and operations; it is not the Institutional Comet implementation.

05 / METHOD & LIMITS

Evidence before inference.

This is an independent community research artifact, not an official Compound interface. It does not connect to a wallet or construct transactions.

The question here is technical revocability. Authority to develop a product, governance policy, legal duties and executable contract permissions are different questions.
